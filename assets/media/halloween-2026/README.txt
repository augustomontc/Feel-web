Feel — Halloween 2026 press kit
================================

Feel is a weather app that writes the day as a sentence about your body rather
than showing a number. Version 1.11 adds a seven-scene Halloween collection,
free for the month of October 2026.

event-video/
------------
The two In-App Event placements, byte-identical to the files submitted to
App Store Connect. Both are text-free, silent, seamless 8-second loops at
30 fps, rendered from the app's own background stack.

  harvest-village-event-card-1920x1080.mp4       event card, 16:9
  harvest-village-event-details-1080x1920.mp4    event details, 9:16
  *-first-frame.png                              poster stills
  validation.json                                encode and loop-seam record


scenes/
-------
All seven scenes of the Halloween collection, each at day, evening and night
(21 files, 1440 x 1440 PNG).

Every Feel scene is painted once in neutral daylight and given two companion
light layers the app composites at dusk and after dark, so a scene is three
looks rather than three pictures. These stills are rendered through the app's
own background stack, not exported from the asset catalog.

  halloween-<scene>-day.png
  halloween-<scene>-evening.png
  halloween-<scene>-night.png

Scenes, in the order the in-app picker shows them: harvest-village,
pumpkin-patch, covered-bridge, witchs-cottage, hollow-woods, haunted-manor,
old-cemetery.
